# Session Zero

Date: 2026-10-01

## Summary

Beyond the Spine of the World lay Icewind Dale — a land trapped in unnatural darkness. For two years, [[Auril, the Frostmaiden]] held the region in eternal twilight. Each night, she flew on a white roc to cast her binding spell. The ten frontier settlements of [[Ten-Towns of Icewind Dale|Ten-Towns]] struggled to survive under her cruel dominion. Desperate residents sacrificed warmth, resources, and neighbors to appease her, while wild beasts prowled and druids terrorized wanderers.

In one bleak settlement, unlikely souls gravitated together. [[Niam Sol]], a wildfire druid and musician, finished a set in a quiet tavern. Beside him, his fiery falcon familiar Teallach perched restlessly. Niam preferred trees to people, but the unending winter pushed him toward company. He soon realized the patrons around him were worth keeping close — surviving Icewind Dale alone was a death sentence.

Nearby, [[Luxenfrix the Second]] — a towering white dragonborn clad in mismatched furs, leather, and chainmail — posted by the tavern door. He avoided company. Experience taught him that distance kept trouble at bay. Sitting by the exit, he traced the sharp edge of a wooden hawthorn totem — his holy symbol — until it drew blood, which he quietly sucked away.

Across the room, [[Ji-rah]], a blue-scaled dragonborn barbarian with curling horns and a bear-hide coat, sprawled across a booth. Tankards and prey remains surrounded him. A fresh beast tail joined his trophy collection as he fingered a small belt bracelet — a memento from the parents who abandoned him.

[[Io Astraea]], a cleric of [[Selûne]] in warm silver robes, entered the quiet room. She scanned for anyone in need. Spotting Teallach looking miserable indoors, she crouched down, extended her wine cup, and offered the bird a drink. A baffled Niam offered help. Io apologized, admitting she was lost in thought. They shared wine, exchanged blessings, and formed an easy, unusual bond.

Meanwhile, [[Baaa Hamut]], a satyr with calico wool, cornered off-duty guards. Yarn in hand, she pitched spools of wool as luxury Angora. Wanting a gift for his wife, one guard paid twenty gold pieces — a fortune — for two spools. Baaa pocketed the coin with quiet satisfaction and eyed her next buyer.

Nearby, [[Ingrid Verdugo]], a sharp-eyed and pragmatic wizard, played dragon chess against Chrisstel, a formidable dwarf with braided beard jewelry. Chrisstel is a long time patron of the tavern with decades of victories under her belt. The two locked into a tense match until both made a fatal blunder — just as a sudden disturbance cut the game short.

The tavern doors burst open. A supernatural wind tore through, extinguishing every fire and scattering chess pieces. Snow and hail swept the floor. Cold clawed at Niam despite the fire on his skin. Luxenfrix threw his weight against the doors, wrestling them shut against the howling darkness. The wind whipped out of the tavern, pulling the door and slamming it closed.

Through frosted windows, the group beheld the Cold Crone. High in the black sky, she rode her massive roc, weaving her spell of eternal winter. The bird's cry pierced the air as the glowing aurora trailed in her wake.

In the silence, Niam called Teallach to his shoulder. The falcon spread his wings, flooding the dark room with warmth and light. Ingrid gathered chess pieces with a spectral hand, and others relit the fires. Auril left behind a chill of hopelessness, but in that tavern, a flame endured.

These six souls — a wizard, a druid, a cleric, a satyr, a barbarian, and a paladin — found each other at the edge of the world. Whatever secrets they carry, can they face the cruel winter together?

## NPCs

### Auril

The goddess of winter's cruelty and beauty, known as the Frostmaiden. She held Icewind Dale in eternal twilight for two years, flying across the sky each night on a massive white roc to cast her spell.

### Teallach

  Niam's fiery falcon familiar who accompanies him everywhere, occasionally causing minor mischief at tables. He is used as a source of warmth and light in the frozen tavern.

### Chrisstel

  An ancient dwarven woman with a salt-and-pepper beard adorned with beads and jewelry in fishtail braids. She is a formidable dragon chess player who spends her nights challenging patrons at the tavern.

## Locations

### Icewind Dale

  A frozen, arctic region at the northwest tip of Faerûn, currently trapped in an eternal twilight by the goddess Auril, who flies across the sky each night on a massive white roc to cast her spell.

### Ten-Towns

  A collection of frontier settlements beyond the Spine of the World, struggling to survive the endless winter. Residents have resorted to desperate measures including sacrifices of warmth and life to appease Auril.

### Spine of the World

  A towering mountain range that separates the northern arctic regions from the rest of the continent of Faerûn, and marks the boundary of Icewind Dale.

### Bryn Shander

  A major settlement within Ten-Towns where the Harper contact Beldora poses as a homeless woman.

### The Tavern

  A quiet, subdued establishment in Ten-Towns where the party members are gathered, seeking warmth and coin amidst the eternal winter. Notable events include Baaa's wool sale, Ingrid's dragon chess match, and Auril's terrifying passage overhead.

## Items

### Hawthorn Tree Totem

  A palm-sized, carved wooden figure of a hawthorn tree that serves as Luxenfrix's spell focus. It has a sharp edge that he habitually runs his finger over when lost in thought, sometimes drawing blood.

### Ji-rah's Bracelet

  A small bracelet that no longer fits the dragonborn's wrist, so he wears it on a loop on his belt. It is a sentimental memento left to him by his parents before he was raised in the wild.

### Mandolin

  A musical instrument used by Niam to perform in taverns and earn coin from patrons.

### Dragon Chess Set

  A gaming set belonging to Chrisstel. A supernatural gust of wind accompanying Auril's passage knocked it over and scattered its pieces across the tavern floor.

## Spells

### Mage Hand

  A spectral hand summoned by Ingrid to help retrieve the scattered dragon chess pieces from the tavern floor after the supernatural windstorm caused by Auril's passage.
