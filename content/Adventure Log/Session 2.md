# A Prayer for Warmth in the Endless Winter

## Summary

The grim chill of [[the Evermelt]] hung heavy over Icewind Dale as the party prepared to depart the settlement of [[Bremen]]. Armed with grave intel from shield dwarf [[Hlin Trollbane]] regarding a serial killer stalking the [[Ten-Towns of Icewind Dale|Ten-Towns]]—a cold-blooded murderer tied to [[Torrga Icevein]]’s itinerant merchant caravan and the gruesome humanoid frost-sacrifices offered to the Frostmaiden, [[Auril, the Frostmaiden|Auril]]—the adventurers plotted their route. Identifying their target as [[Sephek Kaltro]], a curiously well-dressed human seemingly immune to the bite of the tundra, the party geared up with snowshoes and grim resolve, rejecting costly sled dog rentals to brave the four-hour trudge through packed, crusty February-sludge snow toward the nearby walled fishing town of [[Targos]].

Arriving in Targos under pitch-black mid-afternoon skies, the party bypassed the dilapidated squalor of the frozen docks and followed the pungent scent of hay and manure directly to [[Triglio]], the local general store. Inside, they met [[Jestin Hunrae]], an emaciated proprietor with a mangled "strong hand" who bore an uncanny resemblance to a living scarecrow. Jestin confirmed that Torga and her unsettling, heat-siphoning bodyguard Sephek had indeed conducted business at his counter roughly ninety minutes earlier before setting off toward the trade capital of [[Bryn Shander]]. With [[Niam Sol|Niam]] sniffing the crisp arctic air to predict an incoming blizzard due by evening, the party skipped a tavern rest and raced against the looming tempest, trekking another two hours southeast to beat the storm to Bryn Shander's fortified gates.

At the towering southwest gate of Bryn Shander, the guards proved wary of newcomers, raising eyebrows at the heavily armored entourage and questioning ties to the Zhentarim. Io defused the rising tension with disarming kindness and pious sincerity, securing peaceful entry under the cover of a religious pilgrimage to the sun god’s sanctuary. Once inside the packed frontier metropolis, the party executed a calculated divide-and-conquer strategy: Niam and [[Io Astraea|Io]] peeled off toward the House of the Morning Lord, while [[Ingrid Verdugo|Ingrid]], [[Ji-rah]], [[Baaa Hamut|Baaa]], and [[Luxenfrix the Second|Luxenfrix]] marched into the city’s heart to inspect [[Blackiron Blades]], the local smithy and outfitter.

The dual investigations yielded immediate, high-stakes breakthroughs. At Blackiron Blades, shopkeeper [[Elza]] and her hammer-swinging brother [[Garn the Hammer|Garn]] reluctantly confirmed [[Torrga Icevein]]'s monopoly on regional goods, directing the inquiries straight to the North Look inn where Torrga's caravan bedded down for the night. Meanwhile, outside the House of the Morning Lord, Niam used a clandestine lute melody to make contact with [[Beldora]], a fellow [[The Harpers|Harper]] operative deep undercover as a hunched, vagrant beggar who warned that Sephek was likely prowling the snowy alleys alone. Inside the temple, Io offered a heartfelt donation and an earnest prayer to [[Selûne]] and [[Amaunator]], triggering a celestial shooting star that pierced Auril's gloom and blazed with divine clarity. Reunited outside the smithy, the party stood armed with divine portents and hardened steel, ready to corner the Frostmaiden's executioner in the shadowed corners of the town.

## Threads

* **Hunting Sephek Kaltro:** The party has pinpointed Sephek Kaltro to Bryn Shander; with Torga’s crew hunkered down at the North Look inn for the storm, Sephek is expected to be prowling the icy streets alone.  
* **Torga's Caravan & Smuggling:** Torga Icevein maintains an aggressive trading monopoly with dubious, illicit goods; the party must decide whether to neutralize her alongside Sephek or spare her to preserve merchant supply chains.  
* **The Sacrifice Lotteries:** The human sacrifices in Bryn Shander, Targos, and Easthaven are rigged by corrupt local officials taking bribes; Sephek appears to be specifically targeting those who bought their way off the death lists.  
* **Harper Network in Ten-Towns:** Niam has established a secret channel with Beldora in Bryn Shander ("look to the beasts that you cannot slay"), confirming Zhentarim infiltration in Targos and broader political rot across the region.  
* **Approaching Blizzard:** A heavy snowstorm forecasted by Niam is set to strike Bryn Shander by 9:00 PM, cutting off travel and trapping the killer—and the party—inside the settlement walls.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The Road from Bremen

*The party gears up, packs sled-less through knee-deep tundra sludge, and braves the bitter road to Targos.*

- The party finished their long rest at the Sifter’s Pan in Bremen, receiving final advice from Hulin Trollbane regarding Sephek Kaltro and Torga Icevein’s caravan.  
- The party debated renting dog sleds at inflated disaster rates (6 gold per sled), ultimately choosing to march on foot with snowshoes across difficult snowy terrain.  
- The GM distributed rations to the party inventory; Io confirmed *Create or Destroy Water* was prepared, securing party supplies.  
- Everyone rolled DC 5 Constitution saving throws against the freezing march; the entire party passed effortlessly, navigating the crunchy February sludge in four hours.

### Scene 2: Probing Triglio in Targos

*In search of merchant gossip, the party shakes down a mangled shopkeeper and races an incoming blizzard.*

- Arriving in Targos around 2:00 PM, the party followed Baaa’s animal nose (hay and manure) toward Triglio, the immaculately kept local trading post.  
- The party met proprietor Jestin Hunrae, a skeletal man with a mangled "strong hand," who admitted that Torga and her unnervingly cold bodyguard had departed ninety minutes prior.  
- Lux attempted to offer ominous commentary from the back aisles, rolling a 17 on Stealth.  
- Jestin explained that Sephek radiated unnatural chills and mentioned the rigged humanoid sacrifice lotteries across Ten-Towns.  
- Niam rolled a 24 on Survival, sensing a massive snowstorm due to strike by 9:00 PM; the party decided to push forward immediately on a two-hour march to Bryn Shander.

### Scene 3: The Gates of Bryn Shander

*Io disarms suspicious city gatekeepers with unyielding warmth, securing entry into the capital of the Ten-Towns.*

- Arriving at the southwest gate of Bryn Shander at 5:00 PM, the guards halted the party, suspecting them of being Zhentarim mercenaries looking for Torga.  
- Io stepped forward, claiming they were an escort guiding her on a holy pilgrimage to the House of the Morning Lord.  
- Io rolled Insight with advantage (16), speaking genuine words of kindness that softened the hardened frontier guards and granted the party safe passage.  
- The party agreed to split up: Niam and Io headed toward the temple, while Ingrid, Ji-rah, Baaa, and Lux pushed toward the market square.

### Scene 4: Secrets of the Morning Lord & Blackiron Blades

*A clandestine Harper rendezvous and a divine prayer unfold while the frontline squad tracks Torga to the local inn.*

- Ingrid, Ji-rah, Baaa, and Lux entered Blackiron Blades, greeting shopkeeper Elza and dwarven smith Garn the Hammer.  
- After Mike rolled a d20 to check for hunt pelts (rolling an empty inventory), Elza revealed that Torga had traded with them an hour earlier and was staying at the North Look inn.  
- Meanwhile, Io and Niam arrived at the run-down House of the Morning Lord, met by the weary sun-priest Mishan.  
- Niam spotted a disguised beggar outside; spending a Story Point and casting *Guidance* for a total of 12 on Perception, he used a lute melody counterpoint to make contact with Beldora, a veteran Harper agent.  
- Beldora warned Niam that Sephek is likely stalking the streets alone tonight and advised extreme caution.  
- Inside the temple, Io donated 10 GP and rolled a 24 on Religion, uttering a heartfelt prayer that summoned a shooting star forming Amaunator's Belt.  
- The divine omen granted Io awareness of a cold, malevolent entity in northern Bryn Shander, along with a free casting of *Guiding Bolt*.  
- The party reunited outside the smithy, converging on their target at the North Look inn.

## NPCs

### Sephek Kaltro

  A tall, strikingly well-dressed human merchant-guard who strolls through the arctic wastes without cold-weather gear or cloaks. He serves as Torga Icevein’s primary enforcer and is suspected of being the serial killer brutally executing lottery dodgers across the Ten-Towns. He radiates an unnatural, soul-sapping frost and is believed to be prowling Bryn Shander’s streets alone under the cover of the incoming storm.

### Torrga Icevein

  A ruthless, opportunistic dwarf merchant who commands "Torg's," a traveling caravan of sleds hauling goods across the Ten-Towns. While not confirmed to be a cultist herself, she is widely feared for shady dealings, cutthroat pricing, and possible ties to the Zhentarim. She is currently boarded at the North Look inn in Bryn Shander.

### Jestin Hunrae

  The skeletal, gaunt human proprietor of Triglio General Store in Targos, boasting a withered and mangled left hand he jokingly calls his "strong hand." He lost use of his hand in a boating accident with a rival vessel from Termalaine and lives in terror of Torga and her creepy bodyguard. He provided the party with crucial timeline intel regarding Torga's trek toward Bryn Shander.

### Beldora

  A cunning human Harper operative stationed in Bryn Shander, masquerading as a hunched, cross-eyed, and ragged homeless woman outside the House of the Morning Lord. She answered Niam’s secret musical counterpoint and shared confidential intel regarding Zhentarim activity in Targos, Torga's movements, and the unnatural, magical nature of Sephek Kaltro.

### Miishan

  A dark-skinned, bald, and weary priest of Amaunator tending to the faded, drafty sanctuary of the House of the Morning Lord in Bryn Shander. Sapped of spirit after two years of ceaseless twilight, his waning faith was deeply revitalized by Io’s respectful presence, generous 10 GP donation, and moving prayer.

### Elza & Garn the Hammer

  A pair of shield dwarf siblings operating Blackiron Blades in Bryn Shander; Garn hammers out steel in the smithy while Elza manages adventuring gear and supplies in the front shop. Elza is an old trading contact of Ji-rah who harbors profound resentment and fear toward Torga's coercive trade monopoly.

## Locations

### Targos

  The second-largest settlement in the Ten-Towns, home to around 1,000 residents and a grim, frozen fleet of fishing vessels on Maer Dualdon. Governed by Town Speaker Naerth Maxildanarr, it has begun participating in monthly humanoid frost-sacrifices to appease Auril and is rumored to harbor deep Zhentarim influence.

### Triglio

  The only surviving, well-maintained general store operating in Targos, recognizable by its wooden sign depicting three fish and a musical note. It is run by Jestin Hunrae and serves as Torga Icevein's mandatory trading drop-off point in town.

### Bryn Shander

  The fortified, circular capital and trade hub of the Ten-Towns, perched atop a frozen hill and protected by high wooden palisades, stone gates, and an organized militia. Housing over 1,200 stressed souls, the town center is divided between the bustling town hall square, the Blackiron Blades smithy, the House of the Morning Lord, and the North Look tavern.

### House of the Morning Lord

  A weathered shrine in Bryn Shander dedicated to Amaunator, the sun god (often conflated with Lathander by southern travelers). Presided over by the priest Mishan, the temple has fallen into disrepair during the endless winter, serving as a sanctuary for desperate commoners and the backdrop for Io’s miraculous shooting star vision.

### Blackiron Blades

  A renowned smithy and outfitting post situated on the north side of Bryn Shander’s town square, run by Garn the Hammer and his sister Elza. It offers basic weapons, gear, and repairs to travelers and served as the party’s primary lead in tracking Torga to the North Look.
