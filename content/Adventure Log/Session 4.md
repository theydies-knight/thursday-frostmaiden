# Served Ice Cold

## Summary

The howling blizzard outside the Northlook Inn served as the backdrop for a deadly confrontation on two fronts. In the northern alleyway, [[Ji-rah]] and [[Io Astraea|Io]] faced off against the unnaturally resilient [[Sephek Kaltro]]—who stood unfazed by the freezing temperatures in nothing but a vest and trousers—and his pair of summoned ice mephits. Meanwhile, just outside the tavern's threshold, [[Niam Sol|Niam]]’s ruse of selling fresh owlbear pelts and meat had completely unraveled. Surrounded by the dwarven merchant [[Torrga Icevein]] and her four armed thugs, Niam narrowly dodged a blade aimed at his throat just as [[Baaa Hamut|Baaa]], [[Luxenfrix the Second|Luxenfrix]], and [[Ingrid Verdugo|Ingrid]] burst from the warmth of the inn to join the fray.

The skirmish quickly erupted into a display of chaotic magic and brute force. Baaa immediately set the tone by bleating an _Eldritch Blast_ that manifested as the physical letters "E-I-E-I-O," crushing one of Torrga bandits and exploding him into a pile of hay. Torrga retaliated by firing a pistol into Niam, while her remaining thugs rained crossbow bolts down from cover. Niam answered by summoning his wildfire spirit, scorching the ambushers before teleporting to safety alongside Baaa. Moments later, Luxenfrix charged into the snowy street to cleave another bandit in two with his greataxe, and Ingrid efficiently finished off the last of Torrga henchmen with a volley of _Magic Missiles_.

To the north, the battle against Sephek grew perilous as one of the ice mephits shrouded the alley in a thick _Fog Cloud_. Sephek slashed Ji-rah with a conjured longsword of ice before _Misty Stepping_ through the mist. Rather than deterring the barbarian, the bloodshed sent Ji-rah into an ecstatic rage; he crackled with blue lightning and physically doubled in size, towering like a kaiju over the battlefield as he cleaved through the mephits and unleashed a line of lightning breath. Io called down a _Moonbeam_ to sear Sephek with radiant light, and when she attempted to force him to grovel with a _Command_ spell, a crystalline shimmer of divine magic deflected her will—revealing to the cleric that Sephek was a direct agent of the Frostmaiden, Auril. Sephek retaliated brutally, carving into Io with his ice blade and leaving her bloodied, though she stubbornly maintained her concentration on the lunar beam.

As the two battlefields converged, Luxenfrix healed Niam’s bullet wounds with a refreshing, wintry _Lay on Hands_, allowing the druid to teleport up the street and blast Sephek with _Burning Hands_. Realizing her men were dead, Torrga attempted to dash away into the storm, only for Ingrid to bend space with _Vortex Warp_ and yank the dwarf right back into the party's clutches. Baaa’s spectral, one-eyed mini-goat _Spiritual Weapon_ shattered the mephit's concentration on the fog, giving the satyr a clear shot to finish Sephek. With another bleated blast of eldritch magic, Baaa transformed the killer into a haystack topped with his own severed head. While Luxenfrix and Niam used _Command_, _Hold Person_, and sheer intimidation to hogtie Torrga, the party investigated Sephek’s remains. With the aid of Luxenfrix's _Divine Sense_, they discovered Sephek was an undead possessed by a winter spirit of Auril. As Io claimed Sephek’s blade—which transformed from ice into a glowing, steel _Moon-Touched Sword_ in her grasp—Niam decapitated the corpse to claim Hlin Trollbane's bounty and set the bodies ablaze in a sacred pyre, banishing the winter spirit for good.

## Threads

### Torrga Icevein's Interrogation

The party successfully captured Torrga Icevein alive and hogtied her. Niam and the others intend to interrogate her to find out what she knows about Sephek's operation and to disrupt the local black market in Ten-Towns.

### Hlin Trollbane's Bounty

Niam severed Sephek Kaltro's head and tied it into a bundle using the dead man's shirt, mounting it on a stick like a bindle. The party needs to return this grisly trophy to [[Hlin Trollbane]] to collect their reward.

### Unidentified Gems

While looting the bodies of Sephek and the bandits, the party found a small pouch containing five non-magical gems that will need to be appraised or identified to determine their gold value.

### Agents of the Frostmaiden

Through a high Religion check and Luxenfrix's _Divine Sense_, the party learned that Sephek Kaltro was actually a dead man whose corpse was inhabited by a winter spirit bound to Auril. The party must remain vigilant for other undead "wights" or possessed agents operating on behalf of the Frostmaiden.

## Scenes

### Ambush Outside the Northlook

_The party is split between two simultaneous skirmishes in the blizzard outside the Northlook Inn as they fight Torrga bandits and Sephek Kaltro._

- Io used her _Vigilant Blessing_ feature before initiative to grant Ji-rah advantage on his roll, while Baaa rolled a natural 20 and Ingrid rolled a natural 1.
- Stepping out of the inn, Baaa heard Niam yell that Torrga thugs were trying to steal his "meat" and kill him. Baaa cast _Eldritch Blast_ (rolling an 18 to hit) at Bandit C, dealing 13 force damage. The blast manifested as the physical letters "E-I-E-I-O," crushing the bandit and exploding him into a pile of hay.
- Bandit F threw an incendiary or alchemical attack at Niam, who succeeded on a DC 11 Dexterity saving throw (rolling a 12) to take no damage and avoid catching fire.
- Torrga Icevein backed up and fired her pistol at Niam, hitting on an 18 for 6 piercing damage. Using the weapon's Vex mastery, she fired a second shot with advantage, which missed.
- Up north, Sephek Kaltro rushed Ji-rah and rolled two natural 1s on his attacks. The GM spent a Story Point to reroll, hitting Ji-rah for 17 total damage (12 slashing, 5 cold) before Sephek used _Misty Step_ to teleport away.
- Bandit B shot Niam with a crossbow (rolling a 22 to hit) for 4 piercing damage and ducked behind cover.
- Thrilled by the bloodshed, Ji-rah entered a _Rage_, glowing with blue lightning and growing from Medium to Large size. He made a _Reckless Attack_ with his greataxe against Sephek (rolling a 22 to hit) and dealt 12 slashing damage.
- Io cast _Moonbeam_ on Sephek, declaring, "May the Moon Maiden illuminate your sins." Sephek failed his Constitution save and took 7 radiant damage before Io moved behind a stack of boxes for cover.
- Ice Mephit H cast _Fog Cloud_, heavily obscuring the northern alleyway. Ice Mephit D breathed frost on Ji-rah, who succeeded on a DC 10 Constitution save for half damage, though the cold damage still left the barbarian bloodied.

### Turning the Tide with Fire and Frost

_Luxenfrix, Niam, and Ingrid wipe out the remaining bandits in the south while Io discovers the divine source of Sephek's power._

- Luxenfrix exited the inn, charged Bandit A, and rolled a natural 1 with his greataxe. He spent his Heroic Inspiration to reroll, hitting and killing the bandit instantly.
- Niam expended a Wild Shape use to summon his Wildfire Spirit within 10 feet of the remaining southern enemies. The bandits failed their DC 13 Dexterity saves and took 7 fire damage each, leaving the thugs bloodied. Niam's spirit then used _Fiery Teleportation_ to whisk Niam away next to Baaa, dealing half of 1d6+2 fire damage to the two bandits who succeeded on their saves.
- Ingrid stepped out of the inn and cast _Magic Missile_, sending one dart each into the two bloodied bandits and Torrga for 5 force damage apiece. The two bandits died, leaving Torrga alone.
- At the top of the second round, Baaa fired an _Eldritch Blast_ (rolling a 16 to hit) at Sephek for 14 force damage. Using her bonus action, she summoned a _Spiritual Weapon_—shaped like a spectral, one-eyed, long-haired mini-goat—that kicked Sephek in the shin for 6 force damage.
- Torrga fired two pistol shots at Niam, missing both, and retreated behind cover. Sephek missed both of his attacks through the fog cloud and used _Misty Step_ to reposition.
- Io moved out of the _Fog Cloud_, shifted her _Moonbeam_ onto Sephek (who failed his save and took 13 radiant damage, becoming bloodied), and used _Quickened Spell_ to cast _Command_ ("Grovel") as a bonus action. Sephek succeeded on his DC 14 Wisdom save as a shimmer of ice crystals protected him; Io rolled a 29 on a Religion check and realized Auril the Frostmaiden was directly powering him.
- Blinded by the fog and blizzard (rolling a poor Perception check), Ji-rah stumbled out of the cloud and swung his greataxe at Ice Mephit D, dealing 10 slashing damage. Using his weapon's Cleave mastery, he struck Ice Mephit H for 3 slashing damage, though the mephit succeeded on its Constitution save to maintain the _Fog Cloud_. Both mephits missed their subsequent claw attacks.
- Luxenfrix cast _Toll the Dead_ on Torrga, who succeeded on her Wisdom save (dirty 20). Luxenfrix then moved to Niam and used _Lay on Hands_ as a bonus action to restore 5 hit points, which manifested as a refreshing, wintry frost that pushed the pistol shrapnel out of Niam's wounds.
- Niam cast _Hold Person_ on Torrga, who succeeded on her DC 13 Wisdom save. Niam then ordered his Wildfire Spirit to hurl a _Flame Seed_ at Torrga, hitting for 7 fire damage as Niam turned his back and walked north. Ingrid followed up by casting _Sleep_ on Torrga, who succeeded on her save.

### Haystacks and Hogties

 _The party prevents Torrga escape, reduces Sephek Kaltro to a pile of hay, and puts the undead assassin's spirit to rest._

- In the third round, Baaa hit Torrga with an _Eldritch Blast_ for 10 force damage, bloodying the dwarf, and commanded her spectral goat to hit one of the ice mephits, putting it in critical condition.
- Realizing her situation was hopeless, Torrga took the Dash action to flee south off the map.
- Sephek lunged at Io with his ice longsword. His first attack (a 14) deflected off her chainmail—which Io views as the comforting weight of Selûne's love—but his second attack hit for 19 total damage (12 slashing, 7 cold after Io's cold resistance), bloodying the cleric. Io succeeded on her Constitution saving throw to maintain concentration on _Moonbeam_.
- On her turn, Io shifted the _Moonbeam_ back onto Sephek, who failed his DC 14 Constitution save and took 9 radiant damage, dropping him below 25% health.
- Ji-rah unleashed his lightning breath weapon in a 30-foot line; Sephek succeeded on his save for half damage, and an ice mephit retaliated with its frost breath against Ji-rah (who passed his DC 10 Constitution save on a 13, taking 4 cold damage).
- Moving north into range of Io's _Warding Bond_, Luxenfrix cast _Toll the Dead_ on Ice Mephit D, which sounded like church bells and a howling blizzard. The mephit failed its save and took 11 necrotic damage.
- Niam used _Blessing of the Raven Queen_ to teleport next to the action and cast _Burning Hands_ on Sephek, who succeeded on his save and took 6 fire damage.
- Seeing Torrga fleeing, Ingrid cast _Vortex Warp_; Torrga failed her DC 13 Constitution save and was teleported right back to the party.
- Baaa's _Spiritual Weapon_ struck the concentrating mephit (rolling a dirty 20) and broke its concentration on _Fog Cloud_. With Sephek now visible, Baaa hit him with _Eldritch Blast_ and spent her Heroic Inspiration to reroll the damage die, dealing 11 force damage. The blast turned Sephek into a haystack with his severed head sitting on top.
- To wrap up the combat, Luxenfrix expended a spell slot on _Command_ (along with a 17 Intimidation check) and Niam expended a spell slot on _Hold Person_ to paralyze Torrga. Luxenfrix hogtied her with Niam's rope and slung the dwarf over his shoulder.
- After Niam healed Io for 11 hit points with _Cure Wounds_, he used his sickle to cut into the hay bale—which reverted back into Sephek's corpse—and decapitated the body, wrapping the head in Sephek's shirt and tying it to a stick.
- Niam and Baaa both rolled natural 20s on Investigation checks to loot the bodies, recovering a backpack, three _Potions of Healing_, three handaxes, five unidentified gems, and 452 gp worth of assorted coins.
- Drawn to Sephek's ice sword (rolling a 14 on Religion), Io reached for it with a muslin cloth. The ice vanished and solidified into moonlight steel, becoming a _Moon-Touched Sword_.
- With Baaa providing the Help action by dancing around her like a harvest festival pyre, Io spent her Heroic Inspiration to reroll a Religion check (getting a 26) and asked Luxenfrix to use _Divine Sense_. They discovered Sephek was an undead corpse inhabited by a winter spirit bound to Auril.
- Niam set Sephek's body on fire, banishing the winter spirit, and the party piled the remaining bandit corpses together to burn them on a sacred pyre so they could not be raised as undead wights.

## NPCs

### Sephek Kaltro

Torrga Icevein's bodyguard and the target of Hlin Trollbane's bounty. He fought in the freezing blizzard wearing only a vest, shirt, and trousers, wielding a conjured longsword of ice, casting _Misty Step_, and summoning ice mephits. Io and Luxenfrix discovered that he was actually an undead corpse possessed by a winter spirit in service to Auril the Frostmaiden. He was killed by Baaa's _Eldritch Blast_, decapitated by Niam for the bounty, and his body was burned to banish the winter spirit.

### Torrga Icevein

A dwarven merchant and bandit captain with ties to the Ten-Towns black market. She ambushed Niam outside the Northlook Inn using a pistol with the Vex mastery, shooting him once before her four thugs were slaughtered by the party. When she tried to Dash away from the battle, Ingrid pulled her back with _Vortex Warp_, allowing Niam and Luxenfrix to lock her down with _Hold Person_ and _Command_ before hogtying her for interrogation.

### Hlin Trollbane

The bounty hunter who previously hired the party to eliminate Sephek Kaltro. Though not present during the session, it was reiterated that she wanted Sephek dead and specifically requested his head as proof, prompting Niam to decapitate the corpse.

### Auril

The Frostmaiden, whose divine authority and biting cold hang over Icewind Dale. When Io tried to _Command_ Sephek Kaltro, Auril's power manifested as a multifaceted, crystalline shimmer that protected him from the spell, revealing him as her agent.

## Locations

### The Northlook

An inn in Ten-Towns where the party confronted Torrga Icevein and Sephek Kaltro. The entirety of the session's combat took place in the snowy streets and alleyways immediately outside and to the north of the tavern.

## Items

### Moon-Touched Sword

Initially wielded by Sephek Kaltro as a longsword made of ice that dealt slashing and cold damage. After Sephek died, Io reached for the weapon with a strip of gossamer muslin cloth; the ice vanished and was replaced by solidifying moonlight steel that glows in the dark.

### Potions of Healing

The party looted three _Potions of Healing_ from the bodies of Sephek and Torrga bandits, adding them to the party stash.

### Unidentified Gems

A small bag containing five non-magical gems of unknown value was looted from the bodies and placed in the party inventory for future appraisal.

## Spells

### Eldritch Blast

Cast by Baaa three times during the combat. Her first blast (13 force damage) manifested as the physical letters "E-I-E-I-O" that crushed Bandit C into a pile of hay; her second (14 force damage) injured Sephek; and her third (11 force damage after an Inspiration reroll) killed Sephek by turning him into a haystack.

### Moonbeam

Cast by Io on Sephek Kaltro. She maintained concentration on the spell throughout the fight—even after taking 19 damage from Sephek—and used her actions to move the beam onto him across multiple turns for 7, 13, and 9 radiant damage.

### Spiritual Weapon

Cast by Baaa as a bonus action, taking the form of a spectral, one-eyed, long-haired mini-goat resembling Falkor. It kicked Sephek in the shin for 6 force damage and later mauled two ice mephits, breaking one mephit's concentration on _Fog Cloud_.

### Magic Missile

Cast by Ingrid (piloted by the GM) upon stepping out of the inn. She split the three darts between two bloodied bandits and Torrga for 5 force damage each, killing both bandits.

### Command

Cast twice during the session. Io used _Quickened Spell_ to cast it ("Grovel") as a bonus action on Sephek, who succeeded on his DC 14 Wisdom save due to Auril's protection. At the end of combat, Luxenfrix expended a spell slot to cast it on Torrga to force her to drop to the ground so Niam could land _Hold Person_.

### Toll the Dead

Cast twice by Luxenfrix, sounding like church bells mixed with the howling wind of a blizzard. Torrga succeeded on her Wisdom save (dirty 20) to negate the first casting, while Ice Mephit D failed its save against the second casting and took 11 necrotic damage.

### Hold Person

Cast twice by Niam on Torrga Icevein. Torrga succeeded on her DC 13 Wisdom save against the first casting during combat, but Niam expended another spell slot at the end of the encounter to automatically paralyze her so the party could tie her up.

### Sleep

Cast by Ingrid on Torrga Icevein during the second round of combat, though the dwarf succeeded on her saving throw.

### Burning Hands

Cast by Niam on Sephek Kaltro after teleporting into range. Sephek succeeded on his Dexterity save, taking 6 fire damage.

### Vortex Warp

Cast by Ingrid when Torrga Icevein took the Dash action to flee the battlefield. Torrga failed her DC 13 Constitution save and was teleported directly back to the party.

### Cure Wounds

Cast by Niam on Io immediately after combat concluded, restoring 11 hit points to the bloodied cleric.

### Fog Cloud

Cast by Ice Mephit H to heavily obscure the northern alleyway. The cloud persisted until the fourth round, when Baaa's _Spiritual Weapon_ struck the mephit and broke its concentration.

### Misty Step

Used twice by Sephek Kaltro to teleport around the northern alleyway and evade Ji-rah.

### Warding Bond

Cast by Io on Niam in the previous session. Its protective effects (+1 AC and shared damage resistance) reactivated during the third round when Niam moved back within 60 feet of Io.

Niam expended a use of Wild Shape in the first round to summon his Wildfire Spirit, dealing 7 fire damage to three enemies on arrival and using _Fiery Teleportation_ and _Flame Seed_ on subsequent turns.

### Lay on Hands

Used by Luxenfrix as a bonus action to restore 5 hit points to Niam, manifesting as a soothing frost that pushed bullet shrapnel out of the druid's body.

### Blessing of the Raven Queen

Used by Niam as a bonus action in the third round to teleport up the street next to Sephek Kaltro before casting _Burning Hands_.

### Breath Weapon

Used by Ji-rah in the third round to fire a 30-foot line of lightning at Sephek Kaltro, who succeeded on his saving throw for half damage.

Used by Io in the second round to cast _Command_ as a bonus action after using her main magic action to move her _Moonbeam_.

### Divine Sense

Activated by Luxenfrix after the battle at Io's request, allowing him to sense that Sephek Kaltro was an undead creature who had died long before the party fought him.

### Party Coin Loot

From the bodies of Sephek and the four bandits, the party looted 20 pp, 220 gp, 314 sp, and 98 cp (totaling 452 gp in value), along with a backpack and three handaxes.

Mike praised Jojo's DMing style, specifically highlighting how asking "And what is your character feeling right now?" adds massive depth and forces players out of third-person mode. The group also shared wild stories about massive TTRPG tables: Mike and Ash recalled playing at a 12-to-15-person table with a strict 30-second turn timer, Kaylee described a 36-hour campaign finale that merged Saturday and Sunday groups into an 11-player table, and Hannah explained how their alternating Thursday _Daggerheart_ campaign manages 13 players using two co-DMs and Discord breakout rooms.
