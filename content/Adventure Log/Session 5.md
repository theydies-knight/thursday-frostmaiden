# Summary

Following the chaotic takedown of [[Sephek Kaltro]] and the subjugation of [[Torrga Icevein]], the party stood bloodied amid the stinging winds of Bryn Shander. With Sephek’s severed head bagged for proof and four fallen bandits strewn across the snow, the adventurers faced immediate cleanup. [[Ji-rah]] hoisted the corpses over his burly dragonborn shoulders, stacking them into an alleyway for eventual town collection, while [[Io Astraea|Io]] whispered a solemn Selûnitan prayer over their departing spirits. The biting tundra began taking its toll on their cold weather garments, prompting the party to retreat toward shelter, though [[Niam Sol|Niam]] harbored darker intentions for their captive caravan leader.

Determined to pry answers about the Zhentarim and the unnatural cold without scandalizing the devout Io, Niam engineered a distraction with [[Baaa Hamut|Baaa]]’s subtle, eerily perceptive blessing. Dragging [[Torrga Icevein]] into an abandoned merchant shed, Niam subjected the bound caravan boss to a brutal interrogation. Using *Primal Savagery*, acid-coated claws, and a farmer’s sickle, Niam tore through Torga's flesh, interspersing the torment with cynical casts of *Cure Wounds* to keep her conscious. Through bloody gasps, Torga revealed that Sephek had perished long ago before being resurrected by a druid sworn to the Frostmaiden, eventually coughing up a local Zhentarim contact name: Naerth. When Niam departed the bloody shed, leaving Torga bound, the druid felt an icy eclipse stifle his spirit—a divine pall signaling that the Morninglord, Lathander, had averted his gaze from the atrocity.

Returning to the tavern to purchase Ji-rah a soothing post-combat pint, Niam maintained a calm facade until Io insisted on taking hot stew out to the prisoner. Upon their arrival at the shed an hour later, they discovered slashed ropes and an empty space—Torga had slipped her bonds and vanished into the snowdrifts, precisely as Niam had anticipated by intentionally leaving her an escape vector to "kick the hornets' nest." Niam’s wildfire spirit, manifested as an ember-glowing firefly, braved the blizzard and tracked Torga's footsteps northward out of town. After a long rest spent mending shredded furs and resting beneath the grim shimmer of Auril's midnight aurora, the party trekked the frozen expanse back to Bremen.

Arriving at the Sifter's Pan, the party presented Sephek's bagged head to Hlin Trollbane, securing an enriched bounty of 300 gold pieces. However, peaceful respite proved fleeting when the innkeeper, Cora Mulphoon, approached in tears. She pleaded for help regarding her missing son, who had vanished alongside two suspicious tieflings bearing sinister talismans of chardalyn—a fiend-corrupted mineral capable of madness—toward the fortresses of Caer-Konig or Caer-Dineval. Outfitting themselves with winter tents, fresh rations, a diamond for *Revivify*, and a stash of illicit romance novels excavated by Baaa from a local back-alley shelf, the party prepared to march eastward across the icy wastes.

# Threads

- **The Missing Son of Bremen:** Track down [[Cora Mulphoon]]'s son at [[Caer-Dineval]] or [[Caer-Konig]] and investigate the two tieflings influencing him with chardalyn talismans.
- **The Chardalyn Threat:** Uncover who is distributing and crafting fiendish [[chardalyn]] jewelry across [[Ten-Towns]].
- **Naerth and the Zhentarim:** Follow up on Torga's contact, Naerth, and monitor how Beldora and the Harpers react to the [[Zhentarim]] presence in Icewind Dale.
- **Torga's Flight:** Torga escaped north into the wastes; she remains an active enemy with knowledge of the party's involvement.
- **Lathander's Disfavor:** Niam must navigate the spiritual eclipse shadowing his connection to the Morninglord after torturing Torga.

# Scenes

## Cleaning Up Bryn Shander

 *The companions clean up the grisly aftermath of battle and manage their freezing gear.*

- Ji-rah bundles the four dead bandit bodies and heaves them into a back alleyway out of the main thoroughfares.
- Io recites a Selûnitan prayer over the dead; her Religion check (19) invokes the Moonmaiden's grace alongside a distinct feeling of divine judgment over their souls.
- Wear-and-tear rules take effect on cold weather equipment: Ji-rah and Baaa each take 2 points of wear, while Niam takes 1 point.
- Io notices Ji-rah's extensive combat injuries and casts *Cure Wounds*, restoring 14 hit points with a soothing lunar breeze.

## The Shed Behind the Alley

 *Niam extracts vital intelligence through calculated cruelty while the rest of the party relaxes.*

- Niam attempts to stealthily separate Torga from Io's sight; Baaa clocks the deception and psychically fuels Niam’s ruthlessness with a patron blessing (Persuasion natural 20).
- Niam drags Torga into an abandoned shed and rakes her face with *Primal Savagery* (4 acid damage).
- Torga reveals Sephek was slain prior to being reanimated by an Aurilite druid, and that she operates under Zhentarim protection.
- Niam uses *Primal Savagery* again (3 acid damage), casts *Cure Wounds* to reset her suffering, and strikes her with a sickle non-lethally.
- An Intimidation/Persuasion check (17) breaks Torga's resolve, forcing her to spit out a Zhentarim contact name: Naerth.
- Upon stepping out into the snow, Niam rolls Religion (dirty 20) and feels an internal eclipse: Lathander has turned his face away from the druid.

## A Cold Trail and Auril's Aurora

 *The prisoner makes a predictable break for it as the nightly curse sweeps the sky.*

- Niam reconvenes with the party at the tavern, ordering pints and warm stews.
- Io and Niam return to the shed one hour later to deliver food and find the ropes severed and Torga gone.
- Niam reveals he deliberately left her unmonitored to "kick the nest" and flush out her handlers.
- Niam tracks her fresh trail with a Survival check (20) and sends his Wildfire Spirit, manifested as a hovering firefly, scouting north along her path for thirty minutes.
- At 10:00 PM, Auril's magical aurora washes over Icewind Dale in chilling luminescent sheets.
- During a long rest, the party repairs their gear: Niam succeeds on Survival (21), Io mends Baaa’s outfit, and Ji-rah patches his pelts (17).

## Bounty and Broken Families in Bremen

 *The party collects their reward at the Sifter's Pan and takes on a desperate rescue mission.*

- The party makes the morning march back to Bremen across the frozen wastes.
- (Retcon beat): Before leaving Bryn Shander, Niam met secretly with his Harper contact Beldora to deliver the name Naerth, aligning with existing Harper intelligence.
- At the Sifter's Pan, Ji-rah places Sephek's severed head onto the table, prompting a disgusted Hlin Trollbane to pay 300 gold pieces.
- The proprietress, Cora Mulphoon, breaks down over her missing son, who returned hostile from an expedition wearing a chardalyn talisman with two tieflings before departing for a castle.
- History checks reveal that chardalyn is a crystalline substance unique to Icewind Dale, durable as metal and receptive to fiendish magic that warps mortal minds over prolonged exposure.
- Cora describes her son's wood-carved Celtic knot ring, giving Io a mental focus for divine navigation.

## Provisions and Paperbacks

 *The adventurers outfit themselves for a long trek and uncover unexpected reading material.*

- The party prepares for a 20-mile expedition toward Caer-Dineval and Caer-Konig, opting to purchase five winter tents (10 gp total), bedrolls (2 gp), and candles (5 cp).
- Visiting a local trader, the party exchanges five 100 gp gemstones for a 300 gp diamond (suitable for *Revivify*) and 200 gp in coin.
- Seeking escapist fiction, Io searches for romance novels; Baaa uncovers a hidden adult bookshelf behind supply crates (rolling an 18) while happily chewing on discarded book pages.
- The party purchases light novels including *The Paladin's Promise*, *Of Harpers and Harlots*, and *The Merger of Midnight* before preparing to set out east.

# NPCs

## Cora Mulphoon

The weary proprietress of the Sifter's Pan inn in Bremen whose husband passed away years ago. Distraught over her only son's sudden corruption by fiendish chardalyn talismans and his disappearance with two tieflings, she begged the party to search the fortresses of Caer-Konig and Caer-Dineval, supplying them with travel bread, rations, and an ale cask.

## Hlin Trollbane

The retired bounty hunter residing in Bremen who hired the party to eliminate Sephek Kaltro. Grateful though distinctly repulsed when Ji-rah slapped Sephek’s severed head onto her table, she rewarded the party 300 gold pieces for their swift, decisive success.

## Beldora

A Harper agent operating undercover in Bryn Shander as an unhoused vagrant. Met in secret by Niam during the night, she validated that the name "Naerth" matched active Harper intelligence concerning Zhentarim activity in the region.

## Torga Icevein

The dwarven merchant captain running Torg’s caravan. After being captured, she was dragged to an abandoned shed and severely tortured by Niam with acid and a sickle, eventually disclosing Sephek’s ties to an Aurilite druid and the name Naerth before severing her ropes and fleeing north.

# Locations

## Bryn Shander

The fortified trade capital of Ten-Towns where the party concluded their battle against Torg's caravan, interrogated Torga in an abandoned alley shed, and rendezvoused with Harper intelligence.

## Bremen

A smaller lakeside settlement on Maer Dualdon where the party returned to the Sifter's Pan inn, collected their bounty from Hlin, spoke with Cora, and purchased supplies.

## Caer-Dineval

A fortified castle settlement roughly twenty miles east across the tundra, named alongside Caer-Konig as the likely destination of Cora's wayward son and his tiefling companions.

# Items

## Bagged Severed Head of Sephek Kaltro

Carried by Ji-rah in a paper and burlap sack, this gory trophy served as irrefutable proof of Sephek's demise and secured the party's 300 gp bounty from Hlin.

## Chardalyn Talismans

Dark, fiendishly suffused crystalline amulets worn by Cora's son and two accompanying tieflings. The crystalline substance is unique to Icewind Dale, possessing the strength of forged metal but prone to corrupting the minds of those who wear it.

## Diamond (300 gp)

Acquired by the party by bartering three unappraised 100 gp gemstones with a Bremen appraiser, secured primarily as material components for future revivification magic.

## Pulp Romance Novels

A collection of dime-store romance and fantasy paperbacks bought by Io and Baaa, including *The Paladin's Promise*, *Of Harpers and Harlots*, and *The Merger of Midnight*, with several pages chewed and eaten by Baaa as "mental digestion."

# Spells

## Cure Wounds

Cast by Io to knit Ji-rah's grievous wounds for 14 hit points with moonlight warmth, and later cast sadistically by Niam on Torga to heal her acid-burned flesh purely to prolong her interrogation.

## Primal Savagery

Cast repeatedly by Niam to coat his claws in searing acid during Torga's interrogation, raking across her face to deal corrosive damage.

## Mending

Utilized by Io during the party's long rest to repair one point of structural wear and tear on Baaa's frayed cold weather gear.

# Resources

## Cold Weather Gear Durability

A survival mechanic tracking garment integrity (up to 4 wear-and-tear points before uselessness). Ji-rah and Baaa each accumulated 2 points while Niam took 1 point from combat and the tundra freeze; successful long-rest maintenance and *Mending* reduced Ji-rah and Baaa to 1 point and cleared Niam completely.

## Wildfire Spirit

Expended by Niam to conjure a fiery companion in the shape of a blizzard-defying firefly, used to trail Torga's escape tracks northward for thirty minutes.

## Heroic Inspiration

Awarded by the GM to Ji-rah for comedic frozen-head banter, and later awarded to Niam as session MVP for his intense and dramatic interrogation roleplay.

## Loric Inspiration

The party awarded Niam a point of Loric Inspiration for the interrogation scene.

# Boons

A purse of 300 gold pieces granted to the party by Hlin Trollbane at the Sifter's Pan for the confirmed death of the serial killer Sephek Kaltro.
