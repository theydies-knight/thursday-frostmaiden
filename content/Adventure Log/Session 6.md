# Summary

The expedition departed [[Bremen]] with fresh gear and heavy pockets. [[Ji-rah]] traded metal for bloodletting power, purchasing a magical greataxe with coin pooled from his companions, while Io claimed a blade touched by moonlight. Their path led east toward [[Caer-Dineval]] in search of [[Cora Mulphoon]]'s missing son. The journey through the frozen wastes of Icewind Dale promised quiet passage across the tundra, but the arctic frost hid horrors spawned by Auril’s endless winter.

Violence found the travelers on the road past Targos. A coldlight walker, a spectral horror wrapped in blinding light and cracked skin, stomped into view. Ji-rah hurled a handaxe into the monster’s frozen chest before it retaliated with savage force. The undead horror unleashed a blast of arctic doom, striking [[Io Astraea|Io]] and dropping the cleric into the snow in a single strike. [[Luxenfrix the Second|Luxenfrix]] stood firm, channeled divine retribution through his oath, and reflected radiant fury back into the creature's glowing core.

Blood spilled across the ice as the party rallied. [[Baaa Hamut|Baaa]] battered the horror with blasts of eldritch force while Ji-rah unleashed blue dragon lightning and carved deep gashes with his new greataxe. Luxenfrix poured healing touch into Io, dragging the cleric back from death's door before trading brutal axe blows with the walker. Though the monster's freezing claws battered Luxenfrix and blinded the paladin with searing light, Baaa delivered the finishing strike, blowing the creature to cinders with force magic that filled the air with the smell of sweet farm hay.

Rather than abandon the remains to foul necromancy, the companions built a funeral pyre. Io placed coins over the corpse's eyes to pay its road toll to the next world. After a short rest, the adventurers established camp under the shelter of an icy bluff. Ji-rah hunted down a wild moose, Baaa raised a warm fire shaped like a small wooden shrine, and Luxenfrix pitched sturdy shelters. The party feasted, slept without incident, and reached Caer-Dineval by mid-afternoon. The sullen settlement reeked of despair and starvation, cut off from eastern trade routes. While scouting the perimeter, Ji-rah spotted black-robed figures wearing chardalyn amulets emblazoned with the mark of [[Levistus]], archdevil of the frozen hells. With the fortress bristling with sentries, the adventurers made for [[The Uphill Climb]] tavern to plan their assault.

# Threads

- **The Missing Son:** [[Hrun Mulphoon]] vanished alongside cultists bearing chardalyn amulets; clues point directly inside the fortified castle at Caer-Dineval.
- **The Cult of Levistus:** Robed cultists sporting chardalyn pendants honoring the frozen archdevil patrol the settlement and enter the fortress grounds.
- **Starvation and Trade Drought:** The ferry between Caer-Dineval and [[Easthaven]] sat broken for two months, stranding trade, cutting off mead supplies, and pushing locals toward despair.
- **Infiltrating the Caer:** Castle sentries keep regular watch rotations around the stone walls, ruling out sloppy perimeter breach attempts.

# Scenes

## Arming the Expedition

*The party makes final trade deals and checks equipment before marching east into the tundra.*

- Ji-rah purchased an enchanted greataxe (+1) for four hundred gold pieces, pulling three hundred and ten gold pieces from personal reserves and ninety gold pieces from party funds.
- Ji-rah sold his mundane greataxe to vendor stores for thirty gold pieces.
- Io equipped the moon-touched shortsword from the company inventory.
- The adventurers confirmed team supplies: six healing potions, a diamond for resurrection magic, five tents, and multiple bedrolls.

## Ambush on the Eastway

*A radiant specter of frozen malice confronts the adventurers across the open tundra.*

- Ji-rah held an attack action and hurled a handaxe (attack roll: dirty 20), dealing six slashing damage.
- Baaa fired an Eldritch Blast (attack roll: 12) but missed the creature across the wide distance.
- Io dashed sixty feet forward toward the target.
- Luxenfrix dashed forward sixty feet across the snow.
- The coldlight walker scored a critical hit on Io, dealing twenty-seven cold damage (halved through racial resistance from fifty-five) and dropping Io to zero hit points.
- Luxenfrix triggered Rebuke the Violent; the creature saved against the spell (Wisdom save: 15) and suffered twenty-seven radiant damage.
- The walker struck Luxenfrix with its second claw, dealing ten cold damage after racial resistance.
- Ji-rah entered a barbarian rage and fired a breath weapon line, dealing eight lightning damage on a failed Dexterity save.
- Baaa struck with an Agonizing Eldritch Blast for fourteen force damage.
- Io rolled a failed death save (roll: 9).
- Luxenfrix used Lay on Hands to restore eight hit points to Io, dashed into melee, and struck with a greataxe (attack roll: 19) for eight damage.
- The walker struck Luxenfrix twice for thirty total damage and flashed blinding light; Luxenfrix failed the save (roll: 3) and gained the blinded condition, while Ji-rah passed (Constitution save: 16).
- Ji-rah launched a reckless attack (attack roll: 26) with his greataxe, striking for sixteen slashing damage.
- Baaa struck the horror with Eldritch Blast (attack roll: 20), scoring five force damage and destroying the undead monster.

## Rites on the Road

*The companions restore their vitality and consign an unholy corpse to ash.*

- Io cast Cure Wounds on Luxenfrix for thirteen hit points and channeled Healing Hands for two hit points.
- Luxenfrix spent seven remaining points of Lay on Hands on Io.
- Io searched the walker’s garments (Investigation: 15), recovering rosary beads and fourteen gold pieces.
- Io placed two gold coins across the walker's eyes as payment for passage into death.
- Baaa shaped a twig pig effigy while Luxenfrix used a tinderbox and oil to spark a funeral pyre.
- The party finished a short rest and spent hit dice to recover strength.

## Camping Under the Aurora

*The companions cooperate to build a wilderness camp and avoid the biting frost.*

- Luxenfrix secured camp shelter through physical labor (Athletics check: success).
- Io stood watch over the perimeter (Perception check: 18).
- Ji-rah tracked prey with help from a wilderness contact and felled a moose (Athletics check: natural 20, total 28).
- Ji-rah harvested hide and venison meat (Animal Handling check: success), providing fresh meals and exempting the group from ration consumption.
- Baaa constructed a fire pit shrine and coaxed flames (Performance check: advantage pass).
- The company finished a long rest under Auril’s shimmering sky, resetting hit points and gaining heroic inspiration.

## Arrival at Caer-Dineval

*The party enters a starving town and identifies dark fiendish influence.*

- The group arrived at Caer-Dineval in mid-afternoon, noting the heraldry of a watchtower above a knucklehead trout.
- The team made history checks to recall regional knowledge; all failed to beat a roll of ten.
- Io observed the townsfolk (Perception: 19), noting sullen faces, crushed spirits, and an absence of hope.
- Luxenfrix learned through street gossip that the local ferry to Easthaven remained broken for two months, blocking food shipments and mead deliveries.
- Ji-rah spotted black-robed figures wearing chardalyn pendants (Perception: dirty 20).
- Ji-rah identified the symbol of Levistus, archdevil of the Stygian depths, carved into the stone talismans (Religion: 16).
- Io recalled that Levistus rules the sixth layer of the Nine Hells (Religion: 12).
- Ji-rah scouted the fortress walls (Investigation: 16), confirming vigilant sentries and risky stealth approaches.
- The party directed their steps toward The Uphill Climb tavern to secure rooms.

# NPCs

## Coldlight Walker

*An undead monstrosity formed from human remains preserved in freezing frost.*

The creature radiated spectral light and necrotic cold, ambushing travelers along the Eastway. It dropped Io in a single blow before dying under concentrated party fire. Its corpse burned upon a funeral pyre.

## Cora Mulphoon

*The distressed halfling proprietor of the Sifter's Pan back in Bremen.*

Though absent from this leg of the road, her plea drove the current expedition. She seeks the rescue of her wayward son from strange cultists wearing chardalyn pendants.

# Locations

## Caer-Dineval

*A cold, struggling settlement nestled against the waters of Lac Dinneshire.*

The town suffers from crushed morale and trade starvation after its passenger ferry broke two months prior. Guarded stone walls rise above the wooden hovels, currently crawling with devil cultists.

## The Eastway

*The primary snow-choked trail connecting the western settlements to the lake towns.*

The frozen path served as the battleground where the party clashed with the coldlight walker and set their wilderness camp.

## The Uphill Climb

*The local tavern in Caer-Dineval, situated along the slope below the castle.*

The party went to the tavern at the end of the session to get beds, warm drinks, and local gossip.
