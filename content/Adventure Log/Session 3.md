# Summary

In the frost-bitten streets of Bryn Shander, [[Niam Sol|Niam]] and [[Io Astraea|Io]] regrouped with [[Ji-rah]] outside Blackiron Blades. Both spellcasters carried a heavy burden from their recent detours: Niam wrestled with the moral ambiguity of his [[The Harpers|Harper]] contact’s orders, while Io felt a bittersweet knot in her stomach after receiving divine approval from [[Selûne]] and [[Amaunator]] to end a life. Seeking warmth and privacy, the trio retreated to the Northlook, a rowdy tavern dominated by the bizarre spectacle of "[[Ol' Bitey]]"—a massive, taxidermied knucklehead trout enchanted with a *Magic Mouth* spell to sing whenever someone walked past, prompting the drunken patrons to roar along in chorus.

Over bowls of warm stew, rare molasses cookies, and a platter of barely cooked meat for Ji-rah, the party debated the ethics of assassinating [[Torrga Icevein]] and [[Sephek Kaltro]] without a trial. Realizing that local corruption and cultish fanaticism made legal justice impossible, they concocted a scheme inspired by Io's favorite romance novels: they would lure Torrga out of town using the promise of a freshly killed owlbear pelt and meat. They tested the waters with the tavern's retired mercenary proprietor, [[Scramsax]], who warned them that Torrga was just as likely to murder them as pay them, and advised a naive Io to stop flashing whole gold pieces in such a desperate town.

As dusk settled, a soul-bleaching drop in temperature heralded the nightly casting of Auril's endless winter spell. Moments later, Torrga Icevein stomped into the tavern with her entourage, accompanied by Sephek Kaltro, who wore no winter coat and sported an unnatural, icy blue glow in his eyes. Niam discreetly looked through the compound eyes of a fly familiar he had summoned earlier, eavesdropping on Torrga's ruthless complaints about business until Sephek announced he was stepping out for a walk to tend to "other business." Seizing the opportunity to divide their enemies, Ji-rah feigned drunkenness to stumble outside after Sephek, with Io following close behind after casting a subtle *Warding Bond* on Niam.

Outside, a sudden blizzard blinded Ji-rah, causing him to lose his quarry until Io used her divine intuition to sense Sephek's unnatural cold. As they pressed into the storm, the wind blew back Io's hood, revealing an ethereal aurora glowing through her hair, which guided Ji-rah to catch the scent of Sephek's pulsing blood down a nearby alley. Meanwhile, inside the Northlook, Niam played a lively set on his lute to win over Torrga's goons before dropping the bait about a buried owlbear carcass outside of town; despite Niam's terrible bluff, the greedy dwarf took the hook and demanded they go inspect the pelt immediately, forcing Niam to loudly play his lute as they exited into the snow to warn his friends.

Hearing Niam's distant lute over the howling wind, Io and Ji-rah realized they were dangerously split just as Sephek Kaltro emerged from the blizzard before them. Though they tried to cover their tracks by staging a drunken argument, Io's sharp tongue slipped, insulting Auril as the "Frost Bitch" while questioning Sephek's immunity to the freezing cold. Sephek's eyes flared with a chilling light as he revealed that his cold heart was his secret, summoning ice mephits from the storm and calling out to Torrga to join the fray, just as Niam narrowly dodged a knife to the throat from one of Torrga's ambushing thugs up the street.

# Threads

## Ambush in the Blizzard

The party is currently split by roughly 120 feet in a heavy Bryn Shander blizzard. Io and Ji-rah are facing down Sephek Kaltro and his summoned ice mephits in an alleyway, while Niam is surrounded by Torrga Icevein and her goons, having just dodged a surprise knife attack.

## Io's Glowing Hair

While tracking Sephek in the storm, the wind blew Io's hood back, and Ji-rah witnessed an unnatural, holographic aurora glowing through her hair—a phenomenon Ingrid had previously noted in Bremen, though Io herself remains completely unaware of it.

## The Morality of Execution

Both Niam and Io are struggling with the transition from adventurers to executioners. Io has vowed not to strike the final blow despite having Selûne's blessing, and the party must navigate the fallout of acting as judge, jury, and executioner in Ten-Towns.

# Scenes

## Regrouping at Blackiron Blades

*Niam and Io meet up with Ji-rah outside the smithy, weighed down by the moral implications of their mission.*

- Niam and Io met Ji-rah outside Blackiron Blades in Bryn Shander after their respective errands at the Temple of Amaunator and the general store.
- Internally, Niam struggled to reconcile his Harper ideals with the pragmatic need to execute Torrga and Sephek without a trial.
- Io felt relieved to have finally felt the presence of Selûne and Amaunator, yet remained terrified and conflicted about taking a life for the first time.
- Ji-rah rolled an Insight check (17) and immediately noticed his companions were carrying heavy thoughts.
- Niam countered with an Insight check (14), clocking Ji-rah's concern, and suggested they discuss their findings over a warm meal.

## Scheming at the Northlook

*Over stew, rare cookies, and a singing trout, the trio debates the ethics of assassination and concocts a trap.*

- The trio entered the Northlook, a noisy tavern where Niam rolled a Perception check (22) to confirm neither Torrga nor Sephek were currently present, and that the crowd was too loud to eavesdrop on them.
- Niam explained that they needed to eliminate Torrga and Sephek to prevent further sacrifices, arguing that local corruption made a legal trial impossible.
- Io protested acting as vigilantes, though she admitted Selûne had sanctioned the mission, and declared she would not strike the final blow.
- Their grim conversation was interrupted by "Ol' Bitey," a mounted knucklehead trout enchanted with *Magic Mouth* that sang a rhyme when passed, prompting the entire tavern to drunkenly sing along.
- Deciding to ambush their targets outside of town, Io suggested setting up a fake trade deal using an owlbear pelt—an idea she got from a romance novel.
- Io rolled an Investigation check (8) to look for hunting trophies to steal as proof, finding only dusty reindeer heads and an old bear pelt.
- Ji-rah offered to use a piece of owlbear he wore around his neck as proof, and rolled a Survival check (19) to realize that because food is scarce in midwinter, they would have to claim the owlbear was just killed, creating a sense of urgency before scavengers ruined the carcass.

## Sweet Tooth and Scramsax

*The party questions the tavern keeper about Torrga and indulges in rare midwinter sweets.*

- Niam bought a drink and tipped the tavern keeper, a retired human mercenary named Scramsax, while asking for a wealthy buyer for his "pelts."
- Scramsax warned them that while Torrga Icevein had money, she might just kill them instead of paying, and noted she would return to the tavern later that evening.
- When Io tried to bribe Scramsax for Torrga's room number, he refused, though he took a liking to the group and asked his cook, Marie, to bring them warm bread.
- Io rolled a Persuasion check with advantage (20, 2) to ask if there were any sweets available.
- Marie begrudgingly brought out a plate of tough molasses cookies made that morning, overjoying Io, who shared them with Niam and Ji-rah.
- Io slapped a whole gold piece onto the counter in gratitude, which Scramsax snatched up like a viper, warning the sheltered cleric not to flash gold so carelessly in Bryn Shander.
- Hours passed into dusk; Marie noticed Io getting tipsy on wine and brought the table water, fresh bread, slices of roast, and a platter of barely cooked, blue meat for Ji-rah.

## A Cold Entrance and a Fly on the Wall

*Auril's nightly magic washes over the town just as Torrga and Sephek arrive at the tavern.*

- As dusk fell, the party rolled Arcana checks (Io: 2, Ji-rah: 7, Niam: 21). Niam felt the bone-bleaching drop in temperature as the goddess Auril prepared to cast her nightly spell over Icewind Dale.
- Torrga Icevein entered the Northlook with her entourage and Sephek Kaltro, who wore only a doublet, vest, and trousers despite the freezing weather, his blue eyes faintly glowing.
- Using a fly familiar he had summoned earlier via *Find Familiar*, Niam warged into the insect's compound eyes, passing a Constitution saving throw (5) to handle the disorienting sensory input.
- Through the fly, Niam overheard Torrga complaining about taking food from children's mouths just to get people to do what she asks, while Sephek stated he was going for a walk to tend to "other business."
- Five minutes later, Sephek left the tavern alone. Ji-rah announced he needed to "drain the snake" and stumbled outside pretending to be drunk to hunt Sephek solo.
- Worried for Ji-rah's safety, Io used her Metamagic to cast a Subtle *Warding Bond* on Niam before heading outside to back up the barbarian, leaving Niam behind to watch Torrga.

## Luring the Dwarf and Tracking the Phantom

*Niam baits Torrga with a musical performance and a terrible lie, while Io and Ji-rah track Sephek through a sudden blizzard.*

- Outside, a sudden blizzard blinded Ji-rah, who failed a Perception check (Nat 1, rerolled to 2 with Heroic Inspiration) and a Survival check (3), completely losing sight of Sephek.
- Io joined Ji-rah outside and rolled a Religion check (16), feeling an unnatural, freezing presence pulling her to the left through the thick snow.
- Trusting Io's intuition, Ji-rah rolled a second Survival check with advantage and *Guidance* (18); as the wind blew Io's hood back, he saw a holographic aurora glowing through her hair, and caught the scent of Sephek's living, pulsing blood 60 feet down an alley.
- Back inside, Niam played folk standards like "Wild Mountain Thyme" and "Binks' Sake" on his lute, relieving Scramsax by getting the tavern to sing something other than the fish song, and earning 4 silver pieces in tips (1d8) from Torrga's goons.
- Niam loudly mentioned having a wolf and owlbear pelt buried in the snow to sell, failing his Deception check (3, rerolled to 3 with Heroic Inspiration).
- Despite the terrible bluff, Torrga took the bait and insisted they go inspect the owlbear carcass immediately before it spoiled.
- Niam packed his gear and began loudly playing his lute as Torrga's gang escorted him out into the blizzard, hoping the music would alert his friends.

## It Takes a Cold Heart

*The split party is ambushed in the snow by Sephek's ice mephits and Torrga's thugs.*

- Both Io and Ji-rah rolled Perception checks (17 and 17), hearing Niam's lute playing roughly 120 feet behind them through the storm, realizing Niam was in trouble.
- Before they could turn back, Io sensed Sephek approaching. Ji-rah leaned on her, and both rolled Performance checks (Ji-rah: 7 with *Guidance*, Io: 4) to fake a loud argument about Ji-rah being too drunk on Io's tab.
- Sephek emerged from the snow, unbothered by the blizzard. Io tried to make conversation, asking how he stayed warm without a cloak while accidentally referring to Auril as the "Frost Bitch."
- Sephek warned her to watch her tongue, and when Io apologized for letting the cold weather give her a "cold heart," Sephek smiled and revealed that a cold heart was his secret.
- Sephek's blue eyes flared brightly as he summoned ice mephits from the blizzard and called out to Torrga that he heard her coming and asked if she cared for a scrap.
- 120 feet away, Niam heard Sephek's shout over the wind and succeeded on a Dexterity saving throw (13), narrowly dodging out of the way as one of Torrga's thugs tried to slit his throat from behind.

# NPCs

## Scramsax

The human proprietor of the Northlook tavern in Bryn Shander and a retired mercenary veteran. Envisioned by the players as having a pencil-thin Gomez Addams mustache and a bulging, stressed eye like Mr. DeMartino whenever his mounted trout starts singing, he runs a tight ship and demands tabs be paid immediately. He warned the party against doing business with Torrga Icevein and advised Io to hide her gold, though he warmed up to them significantly after their generous tipping.

## Marie

The rotund, no-nonsense cook at the Northlook tavern. Though initially frustrated when Scramsax asked her for sweets, her demeanor softened completely after Io tipped a full gold piece, prompting her to bring the party water, fresh bread, tough molasses cookies, and a massive platter of blue-rare meat for Ji-rah.

## Ol' Bitey

A massive, taxidermied knucklehead trout mounted over the fireplace mantle in the Northlook. Once a legendary monster in the lake of Maer Dualdon before being caught by a human rogue, it was bought by Scramsax and subsequently cursed by a wizard with a *Magic Mouth* spell. Whenever anyone walks within five feet of it, it snaps its head and sings a rhyme, prompting the entire tavern to roar the rest of the song at the top of their lungs.

## Torrga Icevein

A ruthless shield dwarf merchant with a heavy beard woven into braids adorned with wrought iron rings and gold pins. Though universally disliked by local shopkeepers like Garn and Ezra, she is one of the few merchants willing to move goods between Ten-Towns in the endless winter. She took Niam's bait regarding a fake owlbear pelt and immediately attempted to have her thugs ambush him in the snow.

## Sephek Kaltro

An unnaturally symmetrical, handsome man traveling with Torrga's caravan who wears only a vest, doublet, and trousers in the freezing blizzard. Blessed or possessed by Auril's magic, his blue eyes glow in the dark and he claims to possess a literal "cold heart." After stepping out of the Northlook for a solo walk, he cornered Io and Ji-rah in an alley and summoned ice mephits to attack them.

# Locations

## Blackiron Blades

A general store and smithy in Bryn Shander owned by the dwarves Garn and Ezra. The party regrouped outside its doors at the start of the session after learning about Torrga's business dealings.

## The Northlook

The premier—and rowdiest—tavern and inn in Bryn Shander, frequented by mercenaries and travelers. Run by Scramsax, it served as the party's staging ground where they ate dinner, spied on Torrga's gang, and launched their twin ambushes.

# Items

## Guiding Bolt Spell Scroll

A spell scroll provided to Io to represent a pre-cast blessing she received earlier, allowing her to cast *Guiding Bolt* without expending a current spell slot.

## Molasses Cookies

A plate of slightly tough cookies baked by Marie at the Northlook. Because sugar is a rare luxury in Icewind Dale, they brought immense joy to Io, who paid an entire gold piece for them and shared them with Ji-rah and Niam.

# Spells

## Find Familiar

Cast by Niam prior to Torrga's arrival to summon a housefly. Niam warged into the fly's senses to spy on Torrga and Sephek's private booth conversation via its compound eyes and vibration-sensing feet.

## Warding Bond

Cast by Io on Niam using a 2nd-level spell slot and Subtle Spell Metamagic just before she left the tavern. Because she walked more than 60 feet away from Niam in the snow, the spell temporarily severed, though it remains active for an hour if they reunite within range.

## Guidance

Cast repeatedly by Io to bolster Ji-rah's checks while tracking Sephek in the blizzard and during their terrible attempt to act like drunken brawlers.

# Resources

Both Ji-rah and Niam expended their Heroic Inspiration during the session to reroll abysmal skill checks—Ji-rah rerolling a Natural 1 into a 2 on Perception, and Niam rerolling a 3 into another 3 on Deception.

## Metamagic (Subtle Spell)

Used by Io (via her feat/origin features) to discreetly cast *Warding Bond* on Niam in the middle of the tavern without alerting Torrga's crew.
