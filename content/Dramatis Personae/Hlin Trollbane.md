# Hlin Trollbane

Neutral good and bearing a scar across her nose, she fights unarmored with a battleaxe and handaxe and fled to Icewind Dale after making too many enemies along the Sword Coast. Out of boredom and moral decency, she investigated three recent murders ignored by the Council of Speakers and traced them to Sephek Kaltro. Too old and frail to confront him herself, she offers a hundred gold pieces to confirm his guilt and quietly dispose of him.
