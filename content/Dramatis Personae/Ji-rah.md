# Background

Ji-rah was left in the wilderness as a child and grew up "savage-born," learning to rely entirely on his own strength to survive among wild beasts. Clad in a bear-hide coat and adorned with beast-tail trophies, he considers himself an apex predator of the frozen tundra.
