# Sephek Kaltro

After nearly freezing to death on Auril's island, Sephek's fading soul was consumed by a frost druid's winter spirit, which cannot leave his body without dying. Claiming immunity to cold from a Midwinter birth, he joined Torrga Icevein to travel Ten-Towns and kill people who bribe officials to dodge the lotteries. Unafraid of capture, he fights using conjured ice weapons that melt in anyone else's hands.
