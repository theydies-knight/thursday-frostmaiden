# Garn the Hammer

He forges the cheapest weapons available in Ten-Towns while his sister [[Elza]] sells adventuring gear. Veteran mercenaries mock the low quality of his smithcraft, making [[Blackiron Blades]] the punchline of jokes about inexperienced newcomers.
