# Summary

Born in Jindraxia, Luxenfrix survived a childhood caravan massacre by the Bitter Hawthorne Clan. He was rescued and adopted by the peaceful, nomadic Bright Willow Clan, who raised him as family. Years later, Jindraxii soldiers killed his foster family, captured him, and forced him to enlist as a military scout. When he deserted to warn his clan of an impending raid, the soldiers tracked him, executed his clan, and bound him to a willow tree to die of exposure. Rescued on the fourth day by the Bitter Hawthorne Clan, he earned a place among them by surviving near-death before eventually traveling north to start anew in Icewind Dale.
