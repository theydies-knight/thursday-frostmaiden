# Summary

Brought to Faerûn as a child from the Shadowfell, Niam retains only faint memories of his dim homeland. After his parents passed away, he found solace in the wilderness and completed an apprenticeship with a druidic circle that taught him the sacred art of tending the hearth. He eventually joined the Harpers after helping them drive back a malevolent aberration along the borderlands. He travels Ten-Towns as a mandolinist, accompanied by his wildfire spirit falcon, Teallach, and fox familiar, Hogar.
