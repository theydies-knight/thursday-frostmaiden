# Lathander

Macreadus used an undamaged golden amulet of health bearing Lathander's symbol as his holy symbol. When Macreadus's spirit is finally laid to rest, Lathander grants the party a special blessing that restores any member who died within the past day to full health and bestows the Blessing of the Morninglord on those who helped.
