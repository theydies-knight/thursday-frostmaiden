# Ten-Towns of Icewind Dale

Ten-Towns didn't spring up overnight. It started from humble beginnings four centuries ago. Immigrants from all over Faerûn came here in search of escape or adventure and built a modest trade post atop the hill where Bryn Shander now stands. One by one, settlements sprung up on the shores of Maer Dualdon, Lac Dinneshere, and Redwaters. The ever-present threat of orcs and other monsters compelled the poorly defended lakeside towns to turn Bryn Shander from a modest hilltop trading post into a walled town capable of defending all Ten-Towners if and when the worst comes.

Most of the towns contain trace evidence of the immigrant cultures that birthed them. This evidence is carved into houses, statues, and other fixtures. For example, the dinosaur carvings on the older buildings of Good Mead remind folk that many of its original settlers were Chultan.

Residents of Ten-Towns tend to remain indoors when they're not working, since it's so frightfully cold outside, which gives each settlement a deathly quiet aspect. Most people who venture outdoors are bundled up in so much cold weather clothing as to be barely recognizable, and they don't stand around long enough for the cold wind to get the better of them.

Auril's winter spell has caused the population of Ten-Towns to dwindle and has heightened rivalries that have simmered for years, turning neighboring towns against one another as competition for resources becomes increasingly intense.

## [[Bryn Shander]]

- Overview & Location: The largest, most cosmopolitan settlement in Icewind Dale, sitting atop a cold, wind-swept hill. It is the only walled town in the region, built to defend the lake towns against wilderness threats.
- Population & Leadership: Population ~1,200. Led by Speaker [[Duvessa Shane]] (head of the Council of Speakers) and [[Sheriff Markham Southwell]].
- Industry & Vibe: A major trade and supply hub rather than a fishing port. It houses The Northlook (a rowdy tavern for adventurers) and the House of the Morninglord.
- Sacrifice to Auril: Humanoid (monthly lottery).

## [[Targos]]

- Overview & Location: Located on Maer Dualdon, enclosed by a protective wooden wall that extends out into its harbor.
- Population & Leadership: Population ~1,000. Led by Speaker [[Naerth Maxildanarr]]—secretly a senior Zhentarim agent expanding the Black Network's influence—and militia captain [[Skath]].
- Industry & Vibe: Home to the largest fishing fleet and industry in Ten-Towns, centered on knucklehead trout.
- Sacrifice to Auril: Humanoid (monthly lottery).

## [[Easthaven]]

- Overview & Location: A booming frontier trade town situated on Lac Dinneshere. Founded by thieves, it honors its shady origins by declaring pickpocketing legal within town limits.
- Population & Leadership: Population ~750. Led by Speaker [[Danneth Waylen]] and militia Captain [[Imdra Arlaggath]].
- Industry & Vibe: Growing trade and fishing hub featuring The Wet Trout tavern and a prominent Town Hall.
- Sacrifice to Auril: Humanoid (monthly lottery).

## [[Termalaine]]

- Overview & Location: Located on Maer Dualdon and bordered by tall pine forests, it is widely considered the most picturesque settlement, incorporated with Calishite architecture and carvings.
- Population & Leadership: Population ~600. Led by Speaker [[Oarus Masthew]], a young half-orc with political rivals.
- Industry & Vibe: Fishing, scrimshaw, shipbuilding, and gem mining from a nearby cavern complex.
- Sacrifice to Auril: Warmth (no fires lit from dusk to dawn).

## [[Bremen]]

- Overview & Location: Founded by dwarf prospectors at the mouth of the Shaengarne River on Maer Dualdon.
- Population & Leadership: Population ~150. Led by Speaker [[Dorbulgruf Shalescar]], an elderly dwarf whose mind has become addled by age.
- Industry & Vibe: Fishing and panning for gold/gems in warmer seasons. Famous for its Five-Tavern Center, built by five competing brothers.
- Sacrifice to Auril: Warmth.

## [[Caer-Dineval]]

- Overview & Location: Built on the clifftop overlooks of Lac Dinneshere, dominated by a 400-year-old stone castle (The Caer) built by Cormyrean founders.
- Population & Leadership: Population ~100. Officially led by Speaker [[Crannoc Siever]].
- Industry & Vibe: Fishing (suffering due to the discontinued Easthaven ferry).
- Sacrifice to Auril: Food (daily catch hung on racks).

## [[Caer-Konig]]

- Overview & Location: Terraced rows of houses at the base of Kelvin's Cairn on Lac Dinneshere, crowned by ruined castle walls above town.
- Population & Leadership: Population ~150. Led by Speaker [[Trovus]], a heavy-drinking silver dragonborn veteran.
- Industry & Vibe: Fishing and outfitting via Frozenfar Expeditions. Plagued by invisible duergar thieves, with morale kept alive by free half-pints at Hook, Line, and Sinker.
- Sacrifice to Auril: Food.

## [[Good Mead]]

- Overview & Location: Nestled between Redwaters and an evergreen forest, founded by Chultan immigrants whose influence shows in local woodcarvings.
- Population & Leadership: Population ~100. Currently has no leader after Speaker Kendrick Rielsbarrow was slain by a verbeeg.
- Industry & Vibe: Built around a two-story Mead Hall that breeds bees and exports famous honey mead across Ten-Towns.
- Sacrifice to Auril: Warmth.

## [[Lonelywood]]

- Overview & Location: A quiet, secluded settlement on the edge of the Lonelywood Forest and Maer Dualdon.
- Population & Leadership: Population ~100. Led by Speaker [[Nimsy Huddle]], an hospitable halfling who bakes cookies and hosts travelers in her attic.
- Industry & Vibe: Logging, fishing, and scrimshaw. Historically serves as a haven for outcasts and criminals seeking anonymity.
- Sacrifice to Auril: Food.

## [[Dougan's Hole]]

- Overview & Location: The smallest and most insular settlement, located on the shore of Redwaters.
- Population & Leadership: Population ~50. Led by Speaker [[Edgra Durmoot]], a plainspoken trapper.
- Industry & Vibe: Subsistence ice-fishing. Famous for its mysterious megalithic ring, the Twenty Stones of Thruun, and its highly mistrustful, inbred populace.
- Sacrifice to Auril: Warmth.
